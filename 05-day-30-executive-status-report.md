## 1. Executive Summary

The Finance Systems Implementation Recovery Project has completed its initial 30-day discovery, governance, and pilot activities.

The project has made measurable progress toward improving financial reporting accuracy and reducing manual reconciliation activities.

However, resource constraints, remaining mapping discrepancies, and limited consultant availability present risks to full completion within the original 60-day timeframe.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with Phase 3 under a controlled, prioritized scope, subject to executive approval and continued monitoring of resource availability.

## 3. Key Accomplishments — Days 1–30

### Governance and Process Controls

- Established a proposed mapping governance framework.
- Assigned responsibilities for mapping maintenance, accounting validation, technical review, and reporting approvals.
- Introduced a centralized discrepancy register.
- Defined initial data refresh and reconciliation procedures.
- Established a controlled change approval and testing workflow.

### Reporting Pilot

A pilot was conducted for one selected department.

The team reviewed high-impact account mappings, investigated reporting differences, implemented approved corrections, and validated the resulting budget-to-actual report against agreed source data.

**Pilot Outcome:** No unexplained material reconciliation differences remained within the approved pilot scope at sign-off.

### Knowledge Transfer

The implementation consultant provided configuration training to the Senior Financial Analyst.

The analyst successfully demonstrated a supervised mapping update in a test environment.

Additional practice and documentation are required before independent production maintenance can be authorized.
