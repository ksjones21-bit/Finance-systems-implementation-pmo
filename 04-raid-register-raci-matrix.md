# RAID Register & RACI Matrix

**Project:** Finance Systems Implementation Recovery  
**Organization:** Horizon Community Health  
**Project Lead:** Business Transformation Project Lead  
**Project Duration:** 60 Days  
**Status:** Draft — Pending Stakeholder Validation

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes a structured approach for managing project risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies, and stakeholder responsibilities during Horizon Community Health's 60-day finance systems recovery initiative.

The objective is to support timely decision-making, maintain accountability, and prevent unresolved problems from delaying the implementation.

## 2. RAID Management Framework

RAID items will be reviewed during weekly project meetings.

Each item will have an assigned owner, documented status, and required action. Material risks, unresolved issues, and decisions affecting project scope, resources, or deadlines will be escalated to the Finance Director or CFO as appropriate.

### A. Risk Register

Risks are potential future events that could negatively affect project objectives.

| ID | Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Mapping corrections produce inaccurate financial reports | High | Reconcile results to approved source data and require documented testing and sign-off | Accounting Manager |
| R02 | Changes negatively affect integrations or downstream reports | High | Complete IT impact assessments and regression testing | IT Director |
| R03 | Analyst availability decreases during budget preparation | High | Reserve weekly capacity, track utilization, and escalate conflicts | Finance Director |
| R04 | Remaining consulting hours are insufficient | Medium | Prioritize critical configuration support and knowledge transfer | Project Lead |
| R05 | Additional requirements expand the approved scope | Medium | Maintain scope boundaries and formal change requests | Project Lead |
| R06 | Historical mapping corrections alter previously distributed reports | High | Assess historical impact and obtain approval before reissuing affected reports | Accounting Manager |

*Impact ratings are preliminary. Likelihood and overall risk exposure will be evaluated during project initiation.*

### B. Assumptions Register

Assumptions are conditions considered likely or necessary for planning but not yet fully verified.

| ID | Assumption | Validation Required | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | Senior Financial Analyst can provide approximately 10 hours weekly | Confirm capacity with Finance Director | Finance Director |
| A02 | Approximately 20 consultant hours remain available | Confirm contract balance and permitted services | Project Lead |

### C. Issues Register

| ID | Issue | Priority | Required Action | Owner |

## 3. RACI Matrix

**R — Responsible:** Performs the activity.  
**A — Accountable:** Owns the outcome and final decision.  
**C — Consulted:** Provides input before completion.  
**I — Informed:** Receives updates or results.

### Stakeholder Abbreviations

- **CFO:** Chief Financial Officer
- **FD:** Finance Director
- **SFA:** Senior Financial Analyst
- **AM:** Accounting Manager
- **IT:** IT Director / Systems Team
- **IC:** Implementation Consultant
- **PL:** Business Transformation Project Lead

| Activity | CFO | FD | SFA | AM | IT | IC | PL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approve project charter | A | C | I | C | C | I | R |
| Prioritize reporting improvements | I | A | C | C | C | I | R |
| Maintain mapping inventory | I | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| Validate account classifications | I | C | R | A | I | C | I |
| Assess technical change impact | I | C | C | C | A/R | C | I |
| Implement approved configuration changes | I | A | C | C | C | R | I |
| Reconcile reporting outputs | I | C | R | A | C | C | I |
| Authorize material reporting release | I | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| Coordinate project milestones and risks | I | C | C | C | C | C | A/R |
| Approve Phase 3 expansion | A | C | I | C | C | I | R |
| Accept operational handoff | I | A | R | C | C | C | I |

**Governance note:** The RACI identifies overall accountability for each activity. Accounting's financial accuracy sign-off and IT's technical clearance remain mandatory controls where applicable, even when the Finance Director is accountable for the final business release. These controls will be documented in the release checklist.

The RACI requires formal stakeholder validation before implementation.

## 4. Escalation Framework

| Escalation Level | Trigger | Decision Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Operational | Routine discrepancies, mapping questions, or documentation gaps | Assigned activity owner |
| Functional | Conflicting priorities, delayed approvals, or unresolved departmental issues | Finance Director |
| Technical | Integration failures, security concerns, or downstream system impacts | IT Director |
| Executive | Material scope, budget, timeline, or resource changes | CFO |

The Project Lead coordinates escalation, tracks decisions, and confirms follow-up actions.

## 5. Weekly RAID Review

The Project Lead will facilitate a weekly review covering:

1. Newly identified risks and issues.
2. Changes in impact, likelihood, or urgency.
3. Progress on mitigation and resolution activities.
4. Dependencies affecting upcoming milestones.
5. Decisions requiring Finance Director or CFO approval.
6. Items that could affect the Day 30 checkpoint or Day 60 objectives.

Each register will be maintained with an owner, status, target date, and latest update during active execution.

## 6. Day 30 Executive Checkpoint

At Day 30, the project team will present:

- Current RAID status and unresolved high-impact items.
- Pilot reporting and reconciliation results.
- Mapping governance and approval-process readiness.
- Consultant hours used and remaining.
- Analyst capacity and schedule constraints.
- Recommendation to proceed, adjust scope, request additional resources, or pause expansion.

The CFO will authorize the next phase based on demonstrated readiness rather than elapsed time alone.

## 7. Operational Transition

The Finance Director will assume ongoing accountability for the reporting process at project close.

Before handoff, the team will confirm that:

- Mapping responsibilities and approval procedures are documented.
- Internal personnel have received required training.
- Outstanding issues have assigned operational owners.
- Reporting reconciliation and change-control procedures are accessible.
- Material risks and unresolved decisions have been communicated.
- The Finance Director has formally accepted operational responsibility.
