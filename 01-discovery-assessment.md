# Current-State Discovery & Assessment Plan

**Project:** Finance Systems Implementation & Transformation PMO  
**Client:** Horizon Community Health (Fictional)  
**Project Role:** Business Transformation Project Lead  
**Phase:** Discovery & Assessment  
**Status:** Proposed — Pending Sponsor Authorization

## 1. Purpose

Assess the current state of Horizon Community Health's financial planning system implementation, identify barriers to adoption, and establish the information necessary to develop a prioritized implementation recovery plan.

## 2. Business Context

Horizon Community Health is a fictional nonprofit healthcare organization with approximately $85 million in annual revenue, 650 employees, and 12 operating locations.

The organization purchased a cloud-based financial planning platform nine months ago. Implementation has stalled, and several financial reporting and budgeting activities continue to rely on manual processes.

The underlying causes have not yet been confirmed.

## 3. Discovery Objectives

- Determine which implementation deliverables are complete, incomplete, or untested.
- Understand existing budgeting, reporting, and approval workflows.
- Identify manual processes, duplicated effort, and data quality concerns.
- Evaluate stakeholder needs and current system adoption.
- Clarify decision-making authority, project ownership, and available resources.
- Establish an evidence-based foundation for implementation priorities.

## 4. Stakeholder Engagement

| Stakeholder | Discovery Focus |
|---|---|
| CFO / Executive Sponsor | Strategic objectives, expected benefits, funding, priorities |
| Finance Director | Planning workflows, reporting requirements, operational challenges |
| Accounting Manager | Data accuracy, reconciliations, financial controls |
| IT / Systems Director | Integrations, technical dependencies, security, support |
| Department Managers | Budget visibility, reporting needs, user experience |
| Software Implementation Partner | Completed deliverables, unresolved issues, contractual scope |

## 5. Assessment Activities

**Stakeholder Interviews:** Conduct structured discussions to understand needs, concerns, responsibilities, and desired outcomes.

**Process Review:** Document current budgeting, reporting, and approval processes, including manual workarounds.

**Systems Assessment:** Inventory existing systems, interfaces, reporting tools, and dependencies.

**Implementation Review:** Examine the original project scope, milestones, testing results, outstanding deliverables, and vendor commitments.

**Gap Analysis:** Compare existing capabilities with documented business requirements and desired future-state processes.

## 6. Expected Deliverables

- Stakeholder interview summary
- Current-state process map
- Systems and data-flow inventory
- Implementation gap assessment
- Preliminary risk and issue register
- Prioritized findings and recommendations

## 7. Discovery Success Criteria

The discovery phase is complete when key stakeholders have been consulted, available implementation documentation has been reviewed, major workflow gaps have been documented, and findings have been validated with the project sponsor.

## 8. Governance and Next Steps

Discovery activities require sponsor authorization and stakeholder participation.

Following discovery, the project lead will present findings and recommendations to executive leadership for prioritization and approval.

An implementation recovery charter and detailed roadmap will be developed after leadership confirms the project's objectives, scope, resources, and decision-making structure.
