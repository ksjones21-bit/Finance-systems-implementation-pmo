# Finance Systems Implementation & Transformation PMO

**Business Process Improvement | Finance Transformation | Project Management | Systems Governance**

*A simulated consulting engagement demonstrating the recovery and optimization of an underperforming financial planning and reporting system.*

---

## Executive Overview

Horizon Community Health is a fictional, midsize nonprofit healthcare organization that invested in a cloud-based financial planning and reporting platform to improve financial visibility, operational efficiency, and forecasting.

Nine months after implementation began, the organization continued to rely on manual Excel reporting, experienced recurring reconciliation discrepancies, and lacked clearly defined ownership of system changes.

This case study demonstrates how a Business Transformation Project Lead could assess the challenges, establish governance, coordinate cross-functional stakeholders, and manage a phased recovery initiative.

**The central challenge:** How can an organization realize greater value from its existing technology investment without introducing unnecessary complexity or becoming increasingly dependent on external consultants?

## Business Challenge

Initial stakeholder discovery identified five interconnected problems:

- **Unclear accountability:** No formally designated owner for overall implementation decisions and mapping governance.
- **Inconsistent data:** Financial reporting discrepancies caused by potential mapping and data refresh issues.
- **Manual reporting:** Monthly reporting required approximately five business days.
- **Limited internal capability:** Routine configuration changes depended on external consulting support.
- **Weak change control:** Mapping updates lacked consistent approval, testing, and documentation procedures.

These findings informed a proposed 60-day recovery initiative.

## Project Objectives

| Objective | Baseline | Proposed Target |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting efficiency | 5 business days | 3 business days |
| Financial reporting reliability | Recurring discrepancies | Zero unexplained material differences at sign-off |
| Governance | Informal ownership | Approved roles and change procedures |
| Departmental reporting | No validated pilot | One accepted pilot by Day 30 |
| Internal capability | Consultant-dependent | Trained and authorized internal staff |

## Transformation Approach

### Phase 1 — Discovery & Prioritization (Days 1–15)

Assess current-state reporting workflows, interview stakeholders, identify data governance weaknesses, establish accountability, and prioritize discrepancies based on business impact.

### Phase 2 — Pilot & Validation (Days 16–30)

Correct high-priority mappings, establish reconciliation controls, conduct technical testing, train internal staff, and validate a departmental budget-to-actual report.

**Day 30 Stage Gate:** Executive leadership reviews pilot results, outstanding risks, resource constraints, and readiness to proceed.

### Phase 3 — Expansion & Stabilization (Days 31–60)

Subject to executive authorization, extend validated reporting procedures, complete knowledge transfer, document operating controls, and transition accountability to the Finance Director.

## Simulated Day 30 Results

The fictional pilot illustrates how a structured recovery approach could produce measurable improvements.

| Performance Indicator | Baseline | Simulated Pilot Result |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting cycle | 5 days | 4 days |
| Discrepancy investigation | 2 days | 1 day |
| Validated departmental reports | 0 | 1 |
| Material unexplained pilot differences | Not established | 0 at sign-off |
| Internal mapping capability | Consultant-dependent | Supervised configuration training completed |

**Interpretation:** The simulated pilot suggests a potential 20% reduction in elapsed reporting time. Results are limited to the pilot and do not establish organization-wide improvement.

## Project Management & Transformation Competencies

This case study demonstrates the application of:

- Stakeholder discovery and business requirements analysis
- Current-state process assessment and gap analysis
- Finance systems governance and data quality management
- Cross-functional coordination across Finance, Accounting, IT, and external consultants
- RACI development and decision-rights definition
- RAID management and project risk mitigation
- Phased implementation planning and stage-gate governance
- User acceptance testing and controlled production release
- Executive status reporting and performance measurement
- Change management, knowledge transfer, and operational handoff

## Key Transformation Principle

**Successful technology implementation requires more than system functionality. It requires clear ownership, defined business processes, reliable data, appropriate controls, and the internal capability to sustain improvements.**

The proposed recovery approach focuses on strengthening those organizational foundations before expanding into additional automation or forecasting capabilities.
